Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Repmind (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We take your privacy and personal information protection very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains: what information we process, the purposes and methods of processing, storage and deletion rules, your rights, and how to contact us.

The App is a local-first, offline tool: no account system, no sign-up or login required. Your workout data stays on your device by default and is never uploaded to our servers. On first launch, we ask for your consent before providing the full service.

2. Information We Process and Why

We process information under the principle of data minimization:

2.1 Workout and Plan Data (Required for Core Features)

  • Content: workout dates, exercise types, sets, reps, weights, training plans, and progress status.
  • Purpose: recording workouts, displaying history, managing plans, and generating statistics and charts.
  • Storage: locally on your device only.

2.2 Information You Enter Voluntarily (Optional)

  • Content: body weight, notes, and other content you enter in the App.
  • Purpose: recording and displaying it as you request.
  • Storage: locally on your device only.

2.3 In-App Purchase Status (Optional)

  • Content: a local flag indicating whether premium features are unlocked.
  • Purpose: enabling premium features and restoring purchases after you switch devices or reinstall.
  • Note: purchases and payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store. We never access your payment account, bank card, or any other payment information.

2.4 iCloud Backup Data (Optional, Enabled by You)

  • Content: backup files exported from your workout data.
  • Purpose: migrating and restoring data between your devices.
  • Storage: your iCloud account (a service provided by Apple). You can turn it off and delete backups at any time in the App or in system settings.

2.5 Device and System Information

  • Content: basic information such as device model and operating system version (provided by system APIs).
  • Purpose: displaying version and device information in the App and adapting features to different system versions.
  • Note: the App integrates no third-party analytics, statistics, or advertising SDKs, does not collect the IDFA, and does not perform personalized recommendations.

3. Storage and Deletion

  • Location: by default, your data is stored locally on your device (the iOS app sandbox). If you enable iCloud backup, backup files are stored in your iCloud account.
  • Retention: data remains on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the App removes all local data.
  • Deletion: you can delete individual workout records in the App, remove all local data by uninstalling the App, and manage or delete iCloud backups in system iCloud settings.

4. Sharing and Third-Party Services

  • We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your workout data with any third party.
  • Unless otherwise stated in this policy or required by law, we do not upload your data to our servers.
  • The only third-party services used by the App are Apple platform services: the App Store (distribution and payment) and iCloud (backup, only when enabled by you). Their processing of data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

5. Data Security

We protect your information by:

  • Relying on iOS system security mechanisms (app sandboxing and system-level data protection) to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.
  • Strictly limiting data access to what the App's features require.

Please understand that no system or network environment can guarantee absolute security. When you enable iCloud backup, data security is also subject to Apple iCloud's security mechanisms.

6. Permissions and Choices

To enable certain features, the App may request system permissions (as shown in system prompts). You may decline; basic workout recording remains available:

  • Notifications: for workout reminders and the rest timer (requested only when you enable related features).
  • iCloud: for backup and restore (can be turned off at any time in system settings).

The App does not request permissions unrelated to its core features, such as contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photo library.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable laws and regulations, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access and correction: view and edit all of your workout data directly in the App.
  • Copy and export: export your data via the iCloud backup feature.
  • Deletion: delete records in the App, or remove all local data by uninstalling the App.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off permissions such as notifications and iCloud in system settings; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out based on prior consent.
  • Account deletion: the App has no account system, so there is nothing to cancel; uninstalling the App removes all local data.

8. Protection of Minors

The App is intended for general fitness users. If you are a minor, please use the App with the consent and guidance of a guardian. Guardians with questions about a minor's use of the App may contact us using the details in this policy.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be displayed prominently within the App. Your continued use of the App indicates that you understand and agree to the updated content.
